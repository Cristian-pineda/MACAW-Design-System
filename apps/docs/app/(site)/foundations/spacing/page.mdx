# Spacing

A 4 px scale. Tailwind's spacing utilities map straight onto it: `p-1` is 4 px, `p-4` is 16 px, `gap-2` is 8 px. Semantic layout tokens name the distances that recur: `layout.stack-md` (16 px) between form fields, `layout.control-gap` (8 px) between buttons, `layout.card-padding` (20 px) inside a card, `layout.gutter` (24 px) at the page edge.

Controls come in three heights, 28, 32 and 40 px (`h-control-sm`, `h-control-md`, `h-control-lg`), and every interactive target is at least 24 px (WCAG 2.2 2.5.8).

## Rules

- Arbitrary values (`p-[13px]`) fail lint. If the scale does not have the distance, the distance is wrong.
- Components own their internal spacing. Card sections, not custom padding.
- Layouts are designed for 1024 px and above (DECISIONS.md #3).

See the [space scale](/tokens#space).
