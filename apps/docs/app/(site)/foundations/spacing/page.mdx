A 4 px scale. Tailwind's spacing utilities map straight onto it: `p-1` is 4 px, `p-4` is 16 px, `gap-2` is 8 px. Half-steps (2, 6 and 10 px) exist for the inside of components only (DECISIONS.md #19).

Semantic layout tokens name the distances that recur, and they follow the density mode: `gap-stack-md` between form fields (16 px, 12 px compact), `gap-inline-sm` between buttons (8 px), `p-card-x` and `p-card-y` inside a card, `gap-gutter` between grid columns and `px-margin` at the page edge. Controls come in three heights, `h-control-sm`, `h-control-md` and `h-control-lg` (28, 32 and 40 px; 24, 28 and 32 px compact), and every interactive target is at least 24 px (WCAG 2.2 2.5.8).
