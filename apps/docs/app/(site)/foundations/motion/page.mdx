Restrained. Four durations (`instant` 0 ms, `fast` 100 ms, `base` 160 ms, `slow` 240 ms), three easings (`standard`, `enter`, `exit`) and one named animation, the loading spinner. Colour and border transitions on controls use `fast`; panels that open use `base` with `enter`; nothing in the system is slower than `slow`.

Motion goes only through tokens (DECISIONS.md #20): `duration-*`, `ease-*` and `animate-spin`. Tailwind's other animations are disabled, and lint rejects raw durations, arbitrary motion values and the `motion-reduce:` and `motion-safe:` variants, so reduced motion applies everywhere without component code.

## Rules

- Use `transition-colors duration-fast ease-standard` on controls; do not invent durations.
- The `loading` spinner is the only looping animation, and it stops under reduced motion.
- No motion for decoration. Motion explains a state change or it does not happen.
- A component that moves declares it in its manifest's `motion` block, which drives the playground's Reduced motion control.
