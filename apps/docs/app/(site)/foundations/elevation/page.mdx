**Tone first, border second, shadow last** (DECISIONS.md #17). Regions separate by stepping the surface tone: the canvas, then layer-1 for the TopBar, SideNav, PageHeader and cards, then layer-2 and layer-3 for regions nested inside them. A 1 px line marks the edge where two tones are close. A shadow is reserved for UI that floats above the page.

Four elevation tokens name those shadows (`flat`, `raised`, `floating`, `overlay`): a card on the canvas may be `raised`, a dropdown is `floating`, a dialog is `overlay`. Radii stay at 8 px or less: `shape.control` (4 px) for buttons and inputs, `shape.container` (6 px) for cards, `shape.overlay` (8 px) for menus and dialogs, `shape.pill` for badges.

## Rules

- Do not use elevation to signal importance; use position and heading level.
- Do not nest cards. Nest a layer-2 region inside the card instead.
- Shadows are defined once per elevation; components never compose their own.
