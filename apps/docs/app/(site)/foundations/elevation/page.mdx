# Elevation

Structure comes from a 1 px border, not from shadow. Four elevation tokens exist (`flat`, `raised`, `floating`, `overlay`) and each is a named role: a card on the canvas may be `raised`; a dropdown is `floating`; a dialog is `overlay`. Radii stay at 8 px or less: `shape.control` (4 px) for buttons and inputs, `shape.container` (6 px) for cards, `shape.overlay` (8 px) for menus and dialogs, `shape.pill` for badges.

## Rules

- Do not use elevation to signal importance; use position and heading level.
- Do not nest cards.
- Shadows are defined once per elevation; components never compose their own.

See [radius](/tokens#radius) and [elevation](/tokens#elevation) in the token reference.
