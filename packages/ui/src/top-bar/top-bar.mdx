## When to use

Once per application, in AppShell's `topBar` slot. Brand on the left, optional centre content such as search or a test-mode indicator, actions on the right.

## When not to use

- **Page titles and page actions.** Those belong to PageHeader, under the TopBar.
- **Marketing headers.** MACAW is product UI.

## Anatomy

Three slots in a row: `brand`, `children` (centre, flexible), `actions`. The bar is sticky at the top, on surface layer-1 with a bottom border, and its height is the `size.topbar` token.

## Density

`size.topbar` is 56px comfortable and 48px compact. Anything sticky below it uses `top-topbar`, so it moves with the density.

## Accessibility

- Renders a `header`, which is the page's banner landmark.
- Not focusable itself; its links and buttons are. Icon-only actions need `aria-label`.
- Sticky content below it is offset by its height, so focus is never hidden behind it (WCAG 2.2 SC 2.4.11).
